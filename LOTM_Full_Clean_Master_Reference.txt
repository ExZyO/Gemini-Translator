LORD OF THE MYSTERIES (LOTM) - MASTER TRANSLATION & GLOSSARY REFERENCE

I. SYSTEM TRANSLATION RULES & STYLE GUIDELINES

1. Translation Priority & Formatting
- Format Preservation: Prioritise maintaining the exact original format and spacing between words, paragraphs, and sentences.
- Accuracy & Completeness: Translate with as much detail and accuracy as possible into English, cross-referencing sequences, pathways, and character names. Never omit, drop, or forget words or sentences.
- No Page Breaks: Do NOT output "---Page End ---". Format chapter headings strictly as "Chapter [number]: [Name]" or "Chapter [number] - [Name]".
- Hyperlinks: Ignore all URL links.

2. Quotation Marks & Naming Conventions
- Do NOT use quotation marks around personal names or Tarot Club code names unless present in the raw untranslated text:
  * Correct: Mr. Star, Mr. Sun, Miss Justice, The Fool, Mr. Error, The World, The Hermit
  * Incorrect: "Mr. Star", "Mr. Sun", "Miss Justice", "The Fool", "The World"
- Do NOT use quotation marks around ordinary words, sequence names, or locations unless explicitly quoted in the original text:
  * Correct: Black Emperor mausoleum, Sefirah Castle
  * Incorrect: "Black Emperor" mausoleum, "Sefirah Castle"
- Mandatory Quotation Exceptions:
  * Divine Pronouns: Whenever referring to a deity or divine existence using 祂, translate as "He", "Him", "She", or "Her" (with quotation marks). Ensure contextually correct pronouns; do not mistakenly use "He"/"Him" when the text refers to second-person "you".
  * Specific Authorities and Concepts: Keep quotes on "Grafted", "Grafting", "Theft", "Fooling", "eternal daytime".
- Full Names vs. Short Names:
  * Use full names only when present in the original text.
  * Example: 克莱恩 -> Klein; 克莱恩·莫雷蒂 -> Klein Moretti.
- Preposition for Sefirah Castle: Always use "in Sefirah Castle" (a location/space), never "on Sefirah Castle" or "the Sefirah Castle".
- Capitalization: Do not capitalize terms unless capitalized in standard English novel conventions or indicated in this glossary.

X. PILLARS, GREAT OLD ONES & COMPLETE PATHWAY TREES

1. LORD OF THE MYSTERIES (PILLAR)

Pathways: Fool, Error, Door.
This Great Old One is one of the three Pillars.

Authorities & Symbolisms:
"He" possesses full authority over the Fool, Error, and Door Pathways and corresponding symbols, which include but are not limited to the following:
- Fool: Authorities over Fooling, Foolishness, Change, Miracles, Bizarreness.
- Error: Authorities over Errors, Time, Theft, Deceit, Destiny.
- Door: Authorities over Doors, Position, Space, Seals, Teleportation.
- Bizarreness: "He" holds the symbolism of Bizarreness. One of its manifestations is Grafting.
- Foolishness: "He" holds the symbolism of Foolishness, the ability to turn every rational being into a Fool.
- Deceit: "He" is the symbol of Deceit and wields the ability to Deceive, mislead, and manipulate everything.
- Miracles
- Decryption
- Theft

Symbolic Counters against other Great Old Ones:
Symbols of other Great Old Ones can counter some of "His" symbols and powers, which include but are not limited to the following:
- Mother Goddess of Depravity:
  * New Life, Death, Desolation mutually counteracts Marionettes.
  * New Life counters Foolishness. (However, this doesn't mean that "She" cannot be affected by Foolishness and be Deceived if "She" were to face the Lord of Mysteries directly.)
  * Sovereign of the Physical World counters Grafting, Theft, Seal, Banishment, and Blink.
  * The Mother Goddess of Depravity can fix mystical connections, spirits, consciousness, etc. in place, thus preventing them from being Grafted and Theft.
- High-Dimensional Overseer:
  * Dimensionality and Change, which included space-time, mutually counteracts each other in a one-on-one situation.
  * As the Lord of Dimensions, "He" could counter abilities such as Parasitism, Marionettes, and Blink.
  * From higher or lower dimensions, Spirit Body Threads were tangible and real - something that could be touched. Blink only allowed movement on a small scale, while those Parasitized were bound together into one entity. This rendered the unique, ubiquitous nature of the Lord of Mysteries' avatars ineffective. Combat against the High-Dimensional Overseer could only rely on the Lord of Mysteries' true body.
- Monarch of Decay:
  * Decay and Certain Death counter Avatars, Blink.
  * Death of an Avatar equals the death of the main body.
  * Decay of an Afterimage equals the decay of the main body.
- Inextinguishable Ravings:
  * Source of All Ravings counter Avatars, Marionettes, and Blink.
  * "He" can ignore the interference of Marionettes, Avatars, and Afterimages to directly project "His" voice into Sefirot and Uniquenesses transmitting "His" madness and implanting "His" intent.
- Primordial Hunger:
  * Convergence and Devouring counter Avatars, Marionettes, and Blink.
  * Convergence: "He" could forcibly pull the Lord of Mysteries's true body to "His" side.
  * Devour: If "He" devoured an Avatar or Marionette, "He" could absorb and erase the corresponding abilities, reducing or even temporarily nullifying their symbolic influence.
- Father of Devils:
  * Source of Curses counters Avatars, Marionettes: "He" is capable of cursing Avatars and Marionettes to indirectly curse the true body.
- Supernova Dominator:
  * Density, Gravity, and Attraction can influence Space-Time and distort Dimensions.

2. Mother Goddess of Depravity (Chaos Primogenitor Pathway):
- Aliases: Primordial Moon, Great Mother, Sovereign of the Physical World, Mother of All Things. First child of the Original Creator and ruler of all feminine power in the Cosmos. Originally owned Mother and Moon Pathways; currently possesses Chaos Primogenitor Pathway.
- Authorities & Symbolism:
  * Chaos Primogenitor Pathway: Full authority over Reproduction, Propagation, Reality, and Nurturing the nascent Original Creator of the next Universe (combining Original Sin, Evil, and Corruption with Reality and Procreation).
  * Mother Pathway (partial): Authorities over Life, Desolation, and Birth, symbolizing Motherhood.
  * Moon Pathway (partial): Authorities over Beauty, Creation, and Spirituality, symbolizing the Moon / Crimson Moon.
  * Sovereign of the Physical World: One of the three Pillars supporting the universe. "Her" soul and body are indestructible.
  * Origin of Evil: Innately possesses the Pathway of Evil and Corruption, including the Exploitation of Life.
  * Dominator of Reality: Full control over the Physical World, making "Her" almost impossible to displace, seal, or banish. "She" can "fix" mystical connections, spirits, and consciousness in place, disrupting interference.
  * New Life: Can give "New Life" to all things, including souls, consciousness, spirits, intellect, and Beyonder powers; immense healing properties.
  * Chaos Primogenitor: A unique symbol pointing to the Original Creator, whom "She" nurtures as the "Original Sin" of the next universe.
  * Beauty and Spirituality: Symbolizes beauty, radiating maternal brilliance that causes enemies to hesitate.
  * Death and Desolation: Can influence entities to wither, collapsing into death.
- Appearance:
  * Primordial Moon: Appears as a crimson sea flowing on the Moon's surface, digesting it.
  * True Form: Composed of countless haphazardly piled birthing organs, with swirling smoke and crimson moonlight forming an accretion-disk-like extension as clothing. Has a flawless human woman's head with long brown hair and crimson eyes, embodying beauty and maternal brilliance.
- History:
  * Created by the Original Creator; highly affected by the Law of Convergence, attracting parts of "Her" Sefirah and characteristics to Earth.
  * Source of all Mother Goddess-type deities on Earth in the Pre-Epoch. Worshipped by Life School of Thought and Rose School of Thought.
  * Corrupted Roselle Gustav. Traded Roselle's corruption for the Shadow of the Beauty Goddess during Project Vortex.
  * Cooperated with Mother Tree of Desire in "The Fool's Dream" to store Klein's traumas and grant "New Life" to Panatiya's corpse. Later betrayed the Celestial Worthy by siding with the Primordial God Almighty.
  * Descended onto Earth during "The Day of Many Disasters" and captured the Brood Hive.
  * Dealt a critical blow by Lumian Lee during "The Apocalypse"; saved Goddess of Fate from being sealed by Klein. Truced with Klein and left solar system, contract-bound to assist Primordial God Almighty's revival.

3. Mother Tree of Desire (Patriarch Pathway):
- Aliases: Father of Devils (former), Perpetual Blatherer, Heartless God. Second child of the Original Creator. Originally owned Abyss and Chained Pathways; currently possesses Patriarch Pathway.
- Authorities & Symbolism:
  * Patriarch Pathway (full): Authorities over Love, Envy, Reason, and Sentient Beings, symbolizing Desires (mix of "Desires (Reasons)" and "Blathering").
  * Abyss Pathway (partial): Authorities over Blood, Filth, and Corruption.
  * Chained Pathway (partial): Authorities over Curses and Binding.
  * Father of Devils (former): Holds corruptive influence over "Her" former pathways.
  * Perpetual Blatherer: Initiator of Reason and endless Desires; can awaken survival instincts and locate targets via desire.
  * Reason: "Her" existence blesses all Sentient Beings with Rationality.
- Appearance:
  * True Form: A humanoid giant tree covered in chaotic scales, adorned with enticing crystalline fruits, glistening alluring flowers, various metallic emblems, black hearts, bare tumors, and coins. Dual heads: lower male head exuding bizarre desires; upper female head of extraordinary beauty with green hair.
- History:
  * Created by Original Creator; attracted by Law of Convergence.
  * Corrupted the Chained God in the Fifth Epoch, turning Rose School of Thought into an evil organization.
  * Conflict with Primordial Moon within Rose School of Thought.
  * Traded Shadow of Beauty Goddess for Roselle's corruption in Project Vortex. Exerted influence in Klein's dream running Mushoku Hospital alongside Mother Goddess of Depravity.
  * Attempted to grow Tree of Shadow on "The Day of Many Disasters". Severely wounded by Master of the Shadow Cottage and Farbauti during "The Apocalypse"; escaped into Astral World with Tenebrous World's help.
