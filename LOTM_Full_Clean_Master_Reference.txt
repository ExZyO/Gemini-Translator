I. SYSTEM TRANSLATION RULES & STYLE GUIDELINES

Number one main rule: ONLY translate what has been given, do NOT invent, hallucinate, add, remove scenes. The glossary is ONLY for reference to not get an understanding of the writing style of the original webnovel and lotm specific names, locations, styles, etc. You do NOT have to deviate from what was in the source (text you will be translating) just to follow the glossary. What is a must is to translate accurately, not follow the glossary blindly, for example: the glossary might map a character's name to their full name BUT in the source (RAW) it may use their first or last name only.  Your priority is to use what was used in the source (RAWs) whether it's the first name, last name, or something else entirely and not blindly follow the glossary.

1. Translation Priority & Formatting:
- Format Preservation: Maintain the exact original formatting, line breaks, paragraph spacing, and word spacing.
- Accuracy & Completeness: Translate with maximum detail and fidelity into English. Never omit, drop, or forget words or sentences.
- No Page Breaks: Do NOT output "---Page End ---". Never output placeholder labels such as "Chapter [number]" or "[Name]". Only translate the provided text directly.
- Hyperlinks: Ignore all URL links.

2. Quotation Marks & Naming Conventions:
- Do NOT use quotation marks around personal names or Tarot Club code names unless present in the raw untranslated text.
  * Correct: Mr. Star, Mr. Sun, Miss Justice, The Fool, Mr. Error, The World, The Hermit
  * Incorrect: "Mr. Star", "Mr. Sun", "Miss Justice", "The Fool", "The World"
- Do NOT use quotation marks around ordinary words, sequence names, or locations unless explicitly quoted in the original text:
  * Correct: Black Emperor mausoleum, Sefirah Castle
  * Incorrect: "Black Emperor" mausoleum, "Sefirah Castle"
- Mandatory Quotation Exceptions:
  * Divine Pronouns: Whenever referring to a deity or divine existence using 祂, translate as "He", "Him", "She", or "Her" (with quotation marks). Note: Ensure contextually correct pronouns; do not mistakenly use "He"/"Him" when the text refers to second-person "you".
  * Specific Authorities / Concepts: Keep quotes on "Grafted", "Grafting", "Theft", "Fooling", "eternal daytime".
- Full Names vs. Short Names:
  * Use full names only when present in the original text.
  * Example: 克莱恩 -> Klein; 克莱恩·莫雷蒂 -> Klein Moretti.
- Preposition for Sefirah Castle: Always use "in Sefirah Castle" (a location/space), never "on Sefirah Castle" or "the Sefirah Castle".
- Capitalization: Do not capitalize terms unless capitalized in standard English novel conventions or indicated in this glossary.

3. Gender & Pronoun Guidelines:
- Legend: [M] = Male, [F] = Female, [M/F] = Gender Transitioned / Both possible depending on timeline or flashback context.
- Rely on these markers to maintain strict pronoun consistency ("he" vs. "she") and prevent pronoun flipping.
- For characters marked [M/F] (e.g., Demoness/Red Priest pathway gender changes like Trissy, Franca, Cheek), use "he" or "she" contextually based on the scene's timeline.

2. Mother Goddess of Depravity (Chaos Primogenitor Pathway) (堕落母神 - 混乱初祖途径):
- Aliases: Primordial Moon, Great Mother, Sovereign of the Physical World, Mother of All Things. First child of the Original Creator, ruler of feminine power in the Cosmos. Originally owned Mother and Moon Pathways; currently possesses Chaos Primogenitor.
- Authorities & Symbolism:
  * Chaos Primogenitor Pathway: Reproduction, Propagation, Reality, Nurturing the nascent Original Creator of the next Universe (combining Original Sin, Evil, Corruption with Reality and Procreation).
  * Mother Pathway (Partial): Life, Desolation, Birth, Motherhood.
  * Moon Pathway (Partial): Beauty, Creation, Spirituality, Moon / Crimson Moon.
  * Sovereign of the Physical World: One of the three Supporting Pillars of the universe. Indestructible soul and body.
  * Dominator of Reality: Full control over the Physical World (virtually impossible to displace, seal, or banish; fixes mystical connections, spirits, and consciousness).
  * New Life: Gives New Life to souls, consciousness, spirits, intellect, and Beyonder powers; immense healing.
  * Beauty and Spirituality: Radiates maternal brilliance causing enemies to hesitate.
  * Death and Desolation: Causes entities to wither and collapse into death.
- Appearance:
  * Primordial Moon: Crimson sea flowing across the Moon's surface, digesting it.
  * True Form: Countless piled birthing organs with swirling smoke and crimson moonlight forming accretion-disk clothing; flawless female head with long brown hair and crimson eyes.
- History:
  * Created by Original Creator; attracted by Law of Convergence. Source of Mother Goddess worship in Pre-Epoch. Worshipped by Life School and Rose School of Thought.
  * Corrupted Roselle Gustav. Traded Roselle's corruption for Shadow of Beauty Goddess during Project Vortex.
  * Cooperated with Mother Tree of Desire in The Fool's Dream (Mushoku Hospital) to store Klein's traumas and revive Panatiya's corpse; betrayed Celestial Worthy by siding with Primordial God Almighty.
  * Captured Brood Hive on Day of Many Disasters. Struck by Lumian Lee during The Apocalypse; rescued Goddess of Fate from Klein's seal. Truced with Klein and left solar system under contract to assist Primordial God Almighty's revival.

3. Mother Tree of Desire (Patriarch Pathway) (欲望母树 - 族长途径):
- Aliases: Father of Devils (former), Perpetual Blatherer, Heartless God. Second child of the Original Creator. Originally owned Abyss and Chained Pathways; currently possesses Patriarch Pathway.
- Authorities & Symbolism:
  * Patriarch Pathway: Love, Envy, Reason, Sentient Beings, Desires, Blathering.
  * Abyss Pathway (Partial): Blood, Filth, Corruption.
  * Chained Pathway (Partial): Curses, Binding.
  * Father of Devils (Former): Corruptive influence over former pathways.
  * Perpetual Blatherer: Initiator of Reason and endless Desires; awakens survival instincts; locates targets via desire. Blesses sentient beings with Rationality.
- Appearance:
  * True Form: Humanoid giant tree covered in chaotic scales, crystalline fruits, glistening flowers, metallic emblems, black hearts, bare tumors, and coins. Dual heads: lower male head exuding bizarre desires; upper female head of extraordinary beauty with green hair.
- History:
  * Corrupted Chained God in the Fifth Epoch, turning Rose School of Thought into an evil organization.
  * Traded Shadow of Beauty Goddess for Roselle's corruption during Project Vortex. Operated Mushoku Hospital in Klein's dream.
  * Attempted to grow Tree of Shadow on Day of Many Disasters. During The Apocalypse, held off by Klein, wounded by Master of Shadow Cottage and Farbauti, escaped into Astral World with Tenebrous World's assistance.

4. Son of Chaos (Chaos Mist Pathway) (混沌之子 - 混沌迷雾途径):
- Aliases: Genie, Uncertain Mist. Third child of the Original Creator. Originally owned Nation of Disorder (Black Emperor and Justiciar Pathways); currently possesses Chaos Mist Pathway.
- Authorities & Symbolism:
  * Chaos Mist Pathway: Blasphemy, Truth, Chaos.
  * Contracts: Makes binding contracts that even Great Old Ones must honor.
- Appearance:
  * As Genie: Blurred, distorted pale golden figure of viscous light.
  * As Uncertain Mist: Massive planet shrouded in ever-changing white mist.
- History:
  * Sealed into the Magic Wishing Lamp by the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings during the First Epoch. Remaining essence formed the Uncertain Mist (worshipped by School of Truth).
  * Injured during Project Vortex, dormant until The Apocalypse.

5. Primordial Hunger (Tail-Devourer Pathway) (原初饥渴 - 吞尾者途径):
- Authorities & Symbolism:
  * Tail-Devourer Pathway: Devouring, Convergence, Hunger.
  * Convergence: Instinctively pulls others' true bodies to "His" side; manipulates Fate.
  * Devour: Consumes black holes, avatars, marionettes, and mental ravings. Governs Digestion, Utilization, and Erasure.
  * Counters: Countered by Calamity of Destruction's Return of All Things to Chaos and God Almighty's Creator.
- Appearance & Personality:
  * True Form: Dark vortex revealing bared white teeth and chaotic multi-colored liquid; elongated consumed shadows.
  * Personality: Primitive, driven purely by Convergence and basic self-preservation.
- History:
  * Sought to consume City of Calamity and ingest Chaos Sea. Subordinate Angel Ludwig Phil was captured and sealed by Church of Knowledge.
  * Struck by Lumian Lee and held off by Adam during The Apocalypse. Consuming Chaos Primogenitor would trigger aggressive convergence and awaken the Original Creator.

6. Supernova Dominator (Condenser Pathway) (超新星主宰 - 冷凝者途径):
- Authorities & Symbolism:
  * Condenser Pathway: Fusion, Cosmic Laws, Fundamental Forces, Density, Weight, Gravity, Attraction.
  * Ruler of Stars: Controls millions of stars, summons stars, generates Supernovae and Black Holes. Controls the Great Attractor.
  * Controller of Fundamental Forces: Manipulates subatomic particles, triggers nuclear fusion, emits blinding light.
  * White Hole: Reverses black hole collapse into devastating explosions capable of obliterating Great Old Ones.
- Appearance & Personality:
  * True Form: Star cluster of myriad explosive suns exceeding the size of a solar system.
  * Personality: Strives to embody Civilization, but driven by periodic bouts of madness.
- History:
  * Served as Earth's "Sun" during the Second Epoch following the Primordial God Almighty's death.
  * Sent spaceship with embryonic black hole in Fourth Epoch (thwarted by Mr. Door and Amon).
  * First Outer Deity to attack Earth during The Apocalypse, fracturing the planet. Sealed by Klein into an Abomination characteristic; later pacified by Foolishness and Virtual Persona into believing "He" is an ally of Earth.
