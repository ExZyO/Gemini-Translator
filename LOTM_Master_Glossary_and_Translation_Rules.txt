LORD OF THE MYSTERIES (LOTM) - MASTER TRANSLATION & GLOSSARY REFERENCE

I. SYSTEM TRANSLATION RULES & STYLE GUIDELINES

1. Translation Priority & Formatting:
- Format Preservation: Maintain the exact original formatting, line breaks, paragraph spacing, and word spacing.
- Accuracy & Completeness: Translate with maximum detail and fidelity into English. Never omit, drop, or forget words or sentences.
- Strict Fidelity & Zero Extrapolation: Translate ONLY what is present in the raw source text. Use the glossary rules and lore descriptions strictly for terminology disambiguation and correct term selection. Do NOT invent, extrapolate, or add background explanations, abilities, or lore into the English prose that were not stated in the original Chinese text.
- No Page Breaks: Do NOT output "---Page End ---". Format chapter headings strictly as "Chapter [number]: [Name]" or "Chapter [number] - [Name]".
- Hyperlinks: Ignore all URL links.

2. Quotation Marks & Naming Conventions:
- Do NOT use quotation marks around personal names or Tarot Club code names unless present in the raw untranslated text.
  * Correct: Mr. Star, Mr. Sun, Miss Justice, The Fool, Mr. Error, The World, The Hermit
  * Incorrect: "Mr. Star", "Mr. Sun", "Miss Justice", "The Fool", "The World"
- Do NOT use quotation marks around ordinary words, sequence names, or locations unless explicitly quoted in the original text:
  * Correct: Black Emperor mausoleum, Sefirah Castle
  * Incorrect: "Black Emperor" mausoleum, "Sefirah Castle"
- Mandatory Quotation Exceptions:
  * Divine Pronouns: Whenever referring to a deity or divine existence using 祂, translate as "He", "Him", "She", or "Her" (with quotation marks). Note: Ensure contextually correct pronouns; do not mistakenly use "He"/"Him" when the text refers to second-person "you".
  * Specific Authorities / Concepts: Keep quotes on "Grafted", "Grafting", "Theft", "Fooling", "eternal daytime".
- Full Names vs. Short Names:
  * Use full names only when present in the original text.
  * Example: 克莱恩 -> Klein; 克莱恩·莫雷蒂 -> Klein Moretti.
- Preposition for Sefirah Castle: Always use "in Sefirah Castle" (a location/space), never "on Sefirah Castle" or "the Sefirah Castle".
- Sequence Referrals & Beyonder Titles: When a character or entity is referred to by their sequence title alone in narrative description (e.g. 一位荣耀者, 一名秘偶大师, 一位秘法师), render it naturally with sequence rank awareness (e.g. "a powerful Sequence 2 Glory", "a Sequence 5 Marionettist", "a Sequence 4 Secrets Sorcerer") when it enhances narrative flow and clarifies power tiers.
- Capitalization: Do not capitalize terms unless capitalized in standard English novel conventions or indicated in this glossary.

3. Terminology & Phrasing Corrections:
- Convergence: Use "convergence" (not aggregation).
- Corruption: Use "corruption" (not pollution).
- Cosmos: Use "cosmos" (not starry sky).
- Self-Awareness: Use "self-awareness" (not self-cognition).
- Emperor: Use "Emperor Roselle" (not Great Emperor Roselle).
- Great Old One: Always use "Great Old One" (not Old One).
- Church of Evernight: Refer casually as "the Evernight Goddess" (lowercase "the").
- Church of Earth Mother: Use "Church of the Earth Mother" (not The Earth Church).
- Chained Pathway: Use "Chained Pathway" (not Variant Pathway).
- Genie / Magic Wishing Lamp: Translate as "Genie" (also referred to as Magic Wishing Lamp / The Magic Wishing Lamp).
- Classical Alchemist: Use "Classical Alchemist" (not Ancient Alchemist).
- The Chaos Sea: Ensure appropriate definite article based on context (the Chaos Sea).
